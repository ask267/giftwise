AI Foundations for Managers · Final Project

Giftwise: be the friend who remembers.

Your messy gift notes and birthday lists become a reminder dashboard, plus a team of AI agents that finds a gift that fits the person, the budget and the days left, with a real place to order it.

Archita Kadam

The problem

Remembering in time is the hard part.

Far from familyMy roommate and I kept asking what to get our families now that we live far away. Ideas are scattered in Notes; gifts need to arrive.
Silver cohort cakesOur cohort gets a cake for every birthday. One rep tracks it all in a spreadsheet, all year.
Remembering late costs options$60 budget, our catalog: 31 of 75gifts can still arrive 1 day out. All 75 at 14 days.

Today: calendar alerts on the day itself, wishlists the recipient must build, and ChatGPT lists you retype every time, with no timing and no store.

What's new

Memory + deadline + real shops, in one place.

Starts from what you already keepPaste messy iPhone notes, a screenshot, or a calendar/contacts export. An agent sorts it into people, ideas, dislikes, past gifts and dates.
Remembers across occasions"NOT another hoodie" and last year's gift are saved and enforced in code, with no retyping.
Plans around the deadlineDays left decide which delivery windows are searched. Reminders fire on the last day to order.
Real local shops near themBakeries, florists and cafés near the recipient for same-day gifts, the long-distance case.

Compared against: ChatGPT/Gemini, Amazon Wish List, Giftster, Elfster, reminder apps, store gift guides, DoorDash.

How the agents work

7 agents on gpt-6-luna. The orchestrator and the house rules are plain code, so no model can change the steps, budget or delivery rules. Shoppers run in parallel; each only returns ids its tool showed it.
